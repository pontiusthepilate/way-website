Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

WAY Limited ("WAY", "we", "us") operates the WAY App. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

A phone number or email address to create an account; profile details such as display name, bio, "Which Way" teaser, category tags, and profile photo; content you post — timeline posts, comments, status updates, podcast episodes, and direct messages; and any message you send to our support assistant.

Location

If you grant location permission, we use your device's location to power Nearby search and to show your approximate position to people you're visible to. You can override the location your profile displays, or turn off visibility to any search mode, at any time in Settings > Privacy. We never sell location data.

Camera, microphone, and phone permissions

Camera and microphone access are used for going live, calls, remote camera access (only after the other person explicitly approves), and on-device live captioning during calls. Phone-state permissions are used to integrate calls with your device's native call screen. We do not access your contacts, call log, or photos outside what you explicitly choose to share or capture in the app.

Wallet and payment information

The WAY wallet keeps a Naira balance and a record of transfers between WAY users. Card payments to fund your wallet are processed by Paystack — we never see or store your full card number. We do monitor transfers for suspicious activity (e.g. unusual speed or amounts) to protect users; this does not block a transfer, it only flags it for review.

Automated processing

Some features use automated, AI-assisted processing: screening posted text and images for policy violations before they're published; parsing natural-language search queries; suggesting profile text and categories from your bio; generating podcast show notes and transcripts from audio you upload; powering the in-app support assistant; and ranking connection suggestions. These calls are made to our infrastructure providers below and are not used to build advertising profiles.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the app, under their own confidentiality and security terms — never for advertising, and never sold: Paystack (payments), Termii and Africa's Talking (SMS/WhatsApp delivery for verification codes), Resend (email delivery), Mapbox (address and location search), LiveKit (calls and live video/audio), Expo and Apple (push notifications), Anthropic (content moderation, search parsing, and the support assistant), Voyage AI (search matching), Deepgram (podcast transcription), and Dojah / Apify (business registration checks, for business profiles only). Information may also be disclosed if required by law.

Your choices

You control what others can see through Privacy and Notification settings in the app — including who can message you, which search modes can find you, and who gets full profile access. You can request account deletion at any time from Settings > Account details; this deactivates your account and its identifier (phone/email) permanently, so it can never be reused to create a new account.

Data retention and security

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use industry-standard safeguards to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

WAY is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
